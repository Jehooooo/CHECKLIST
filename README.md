# Baguio Getaway Checklist — Cute Pink & Purple Nailong Edition 🍓💜💛

An interactive 3-day Baguio City travel itinerary checklist (**December 21–23, 2026**) built strictly with **vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** (zero frameworks or external dependencies).

## Highlights

- **Swapped Day 1 & Day 2 Schedule**:
  - **Day 1 — December 21, 2026**: Rides • Botanical Garden • Mansion House • Ukay-ukay
  - **Day 2 — December 22, 2026**: Arrival • Downtown Baguio • Night Market
  - **Day 3 — December 23, 2026**: Igorot Stone Kingdom • Pasalubong • Travel home
  - Includes a one-tap **`⇄ Swap Day 1 & 2`** button in case plans shift on the go.
- **Cute Pink & Purple + Nailong (Milk Dragon) Theme**:
  - Custom pastel blush & lavender glassmorphism cards, Nailong Baguio sticker illustrations, and an interactive **Poke Nailong** mascot cheer button.
- **Smart Interactive Checklist**:
  - Sub-stop checkboxes (e.g., *Baguio Botanical Garden*, *The Mansion House*, *Wright Park* and *SM City Baguio*, *Burnham Park*, *Baguio Cathedral*) automatically sync with their parent tour's indeterminate (`–`) and completed (`✓`) states.
  - Persistent progress, custom extra stops per day, Baguio bag essentials checklist, and personal trip notes saved automatically in `localStorage`.
  - Filter tabs (`All Days`, `Day 1`, `Day 2`, `Day 3`, `Remaining`) with smooth progressive View Transitions and native `<details>` collapsible sections.

## Running Locally

Simply open `index.html` in any modern desktop or mobile browser—no build step or server required!

## Project Structure

- `index.html` — Semantic 3-day Baguio checklist markup
- `styles.css` — Pastel pink & lavender styling, custom accessible checkboxes, animations, and print layout
- `script.js` — Vanilla JS controller for `localStorage` persistence, progress bar, day swapping, and filters
- `assets/` — Custom Nailong Baguio kawaii sticker illustrations
